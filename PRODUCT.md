# Product context

Referral Intake Reference is an operator-facing demonstration of a secure, asynchronous referral workflow on Azure. Reviewers upload documents, observe dual-engine extraction, inspect disagreements, and approve or reject the result. It must never imply suitability for real records without further threat modeling, compliance review, and production hardening.

The primary task is rapid, accountable human review. Public-workspace safety, traceability, accessible interaction, and a clear, enforced boundary between the test-only mock identity fixture and Azure Microsoft Entra ID are non-negotiable.
